==============================================================================
  8. NOTES ON THE SIMULATED PLAYERS
==============================================================================
  * Bully: chooses Push or Pull by whichever leaves the players in reach furthest back on
    average (looking at snakes and ladders); nudges each passed player the worse way for them.
  * Snake Charmer: moves a head only when it is expected to cost the others at least
    1 square(s) more than it costs her (one roll ahead); uses the ability whenever it is ready and worth it.
  * Oracle calls a random number; Struggler picks random failure numbers (neither changes the odds).
  * Items: drinks and stones are used at once (stones hit the leader); bricks go where opponents
    are likelier to step than the owner; sticks are used when worth >= 1 square(s).
  * Stone and cat-charm penalties were not in config.js, so -1 each was assumed (see top of script).
